Not a cladding.
A system.

In the corridors of the SavHotel Mantegna, IconicWall wasn't used as a simple decorative finish.

The system made it possible to organize technical surfaces, integrate removable panels and maintain access to the plumbing service ducts behind the wall.

In a hotel, this makes the difference: aesthetics can't erase function. It has to make it more legible.

Behind the panels, access to the systems

Behind the IconicWall panels were plumbing service ducts requiring access.

The project made it possible to integrate these technical points within an orderly wall, without giving up the possibility of future access.

IconicWall doesn't just cover the problem: it organizes it.

Precision before installation

Before final installation, panel positioning was verified using dedicated mounting jigs.

The jigs make it possible to check alignment, measurements and repeatability before installation.

Across 13 floors, precision isn't an aesthetic detail. It's the method that keeps the system consistent from one corridor to the next.

4 hours

One floor in four hours

At the SavHotel Mantegna, a floor made up of 9 IconicWall panels was completed in 4 hours.

This figure isn't a general promise. It's the result of this specific project, achieved through design, mounting jigs and a controlled installation sequence.

In a hotel spread across 13 floors, speed means less disruption, more control and greater operational continuity.

Why it matters
in a hotel

In the hospitality sector, renovating doesn't just mean changing the image.

It means working on spaces that need to stay clear, durable, accessible and easy to manage.

At the SavHotel Mantegna, IconicWall made it possible to transform technical corridors into more orderly surfaces, maintaining access to the systems and ensuring repeatable installation across multiple floors.

The project also used 3M DI-NOC finishes on the room doors, as part of the overall material consistency of the design.

Here the value isn't just aesthetic. It's operational.
