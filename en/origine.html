Before the system,
there is a way of looking at interiors.

IconicWall was born from years of real, hands-on work on surfaces: construction sites, hotels, retail, technical materials, constraints, tight schedules and spaces that need to change without being demolished.

It doesn't come from a theoretical exercise. It comes from direct contact with spaces to transform, materials to interpret, and solutions to make simpler, more accessible and more durable.

From surface
to system.

Before IconicWall, there was daily work transforming interiors: architectural films, technical finishes, furnishings, walls, hotel rooms, retail spaces and professional environments to be renovated without stopping everything.

Over time, the surface stopped being just the final point of the project and became the starting point for imagining a different system.

The question.

Why does every project have to start from zero?

From this question comes the idea of separating what must stay from what can change: a stable, permanent and accessible structure, able to host surfaces, light, accessories and upgradable functions over time.

The structure.

IconicWall was born as a permanent infrastructure for interiors. The structure stays installed, while surfaces, materials, light, accessories and functions can evolve according to the needs of the space.

Not a false wall. Not traditional wood paneling. Not a simple cladding.

A design base built to accompany change.

The experience.

IconicWall was born from confronting real construction sites, real materials, real schedules and spaces that need to keep living.

Its origin is practical even before being theoretical: reducing invasive work, making technical parts accessible, simplifying maintenance and upgrades, while maintaining aesthetic continuity and perceived quality.

An open
platform.

IconicWall isn't conceived as a closed product. It's a platform that can engage with architects, companies, manufacturers, industrial partners and different materials.

Its value lies not only in the finished wall, but in the possibility of building a supply chain capable of evolving interiors over time.

A clear direction.

Behind IconicWall is the direct experience of Yuri Meneghetti in interior renovation, the use of technical materials, and the search for non-invasive solutions to transform spaces.

The project stems from this ongoing observation: spaces change, but every transformation shouldn't force you to start from zero.
