Material
follows the project.

IconicWall is built with 3M Di-NOC finishes and extends to paint, lacquer, veneer and custom solutions evaluated per project.

The material base

Finishes
3M DI-NOC

IconicWall was designed to work with 3M Di-NOC finishes: a professional material library capable of interpreting woods, metals, stones, fabrics and decorative textures with continuity, precision and architectural quality.

This base gives rise to the system's material language. When the project requires it, the same structure can also accommodate paint, lacquer, veneer and custom-developed finishes, evaluating feasibility, durability and aesthetic result together.
