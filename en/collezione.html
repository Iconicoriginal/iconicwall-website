01 · Flat

The base.

Material continuity and compositional freedom.

Flat is the panel that builds the wall's visual field. A clean, modular and replaceable surface, designed to host material, color and texture without interrupting the design.

02 · Lux

The light.

Integrated lighting, without visible cables.

Lux brings light into the wall without turning it into a visible fixture. The panel remains part of the system, but adds depth, atmosphere and visual orientation.

The surface.

The wall becomes a resting surface.

A thin shelf shaped by the same logic as the panel: discreet, magnetic, repositionable. It adds function without introducing visible furniture or brackets.

04 · Frame

The void.

An open volume, drawn into the wall.

Frame creates a light container space, without closing off the surface. It displays, collects and safeguards while keeping IconicWall's modular design language.
