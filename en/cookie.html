What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that visited websites can save on the user's device during browsing.

They are used to make the site work correctly, remember certain preferences, collect statistics or, only with prior consent, offer more relevant content and communications.

Types of cookies and similar tools

In addition to cookies, the site may use similar technologies, such as localStorage, sessionStorage, pixels, tags, SDKs or other technical identifiers.

These tools may serve technical, statistical or marketing purposes, depending on their configuration.

Necessary technical cookies

Technical cookies are necessary for the correct functioning of the site.

They enable, for example, navigation between pages, security, content loading, and the storage of the user's cookie choices.

These cookies do not require the user's consent.

Preference cookies

The project includes local storage tools used to remember the user's expressed cookie preferences and, in the configurator, preferred or recently viewed materials.

This data is saved in the user's browser and is used to keep the browsing experience consistent.

Analytics cookies

Based on the code currently analyzed, no Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Vercel Analytics or other analytics scripts appear to be loaded on the site.

If non-anonymized or identifier-based analytics tools are activated in the future, they must only be loaded with the user's prior consent.

Marketing and profiling cookies

Based on the code currently analyzed, no Meta Pixel, advertising pixels, marketing scripts or profiling tools appear to be loaded on the site.

Any marketing or profiling tools must only be activated after the user's consent.

Third-party cookies

Based on the code currently analyzed, no YouTube, Vimeo, Google Maps, Calendly embeds or other third-party tools that install cookies or identifiers through ordinary browsing appear to be present.

The site uses a server-side endpoint to forward contact form requests to GoHighLevel when configured. This does not appear to be a tracking script loaded in the browser.
