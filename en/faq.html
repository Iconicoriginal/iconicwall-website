FAQ

Essential answers to understand IconicWall, where it can be installed and how a custom project comes together.

IconicWall is a modular system for turning existing walls into equipped, magnetic and reconfigurable surfaces. Here you'll find answers to the most frequently asked questions about how it works, installation, materials, accessories, costs and the project process.

The IconicWall system

What is IconicWall?

IconicWall is a modular system for turning existing walls into equipped, magnetic and reconfigurable surfaces.

It's not simple decorative wall paneling and it's not a traditional cladding. It's a wall designed to integrate panels, finishes, accessories, functional elements and, where planned, lighting.

How is it different from traditional wall paneling?

Traditional wall paneling covers the wall in a mostly fixed, decorative way.

IconicWall adds a system logic: the wall can be composed, updated and reconfigured through modules, panels and accessories. This allows finishes, functions and configuration to be changed over time without turning every change into a new construction job.

Is IconicWall a standard product or made to measure?

IconicWall is built on modular logic, but it's adapted to the real project.

Dimensions, finishes, composition, accessories and integrations are defined based on the space, the intended use and the aesthetic goal. It's not a product to simply choose from a catalog: it's a system to be designed.

Are the panels removable?

Yes. The system is designed to allow elements to be removed, replaced or reconfigured, according to the chosen configuration.

This makes it possible to update a wall over time, replace a finish, modify an accessory or adapt the space to new needs.

Is IconicWall magnetic?

Yes. IconicWall uses a magnetic logic to make the wall equippable and reconfigurable.

The technical composition is defined based on the project, the existing substrate, panel dimensions, planned accessories and required performance.

Installation

Where can IconicWall be installed?

IconicWall can be installed in residential settings, hotels, shops, showrooms, offices, reception areas, kitchens, bedrooms, corridors and contract spaces.

It's especially useful when a wall needs to become an active part of the project, not just a decorative backdrop.

Does the existing wall need to be demolished?

In most cases, no.

IconicWall is designed to work on existing surfaces, reducing demolition, invasive work and construction time. Feasibility must still be verified case by case, since every wall has different conditions.

What substrates can it be installed on?

The wall must be evaluated based on flatness, solidity, existing materials, presence of systems (plumbing/electrical), humidity, dimensions and intended use.

A technical check of the substrate is always required before defining a final project.

How long does installation take?

It depends on the wall's dimensions, the complexity of the composition, the chosen finishes, the planned accessories and the condition of the substrate.

One of the system's advantages is reducing time and invasiveness compared to many traditional construction methods, but every project requires concrete planning.

Can it be installed in already-furnished spaces?

Yes, in many cases it's possible to work in spaces that are already furnished or in use.

The operational approach depends on the context: a private home, a hotel room, a lobby or a shop open to the public each have different needs in terms of protection, timing and organization.

Materials and finishes

What finishes can be used?

IconicWall can integrate decorative finishes selected according to the project: wood, metal and stone effects, textured surfaces, neutral colors or more distinctive solutions.

The choice of finish isn't just aesthetic. It changes the perception of the space, the relationship with light, and the character of the environment.

Can finishes be replaced over time?

Yes. Depending on the configuration, panels or finishes can be replaced without completely redoing the wall.

This makes IconicWall interesting for spaces that need to evolve, such as hotels, shops, showrooms and commercial environments.

Are the colors and materials seen on the site identical in person?

Images, renders and staged settings are for illustrative purposes.

Colors, textures and reflections can vary depending on light, display device, the actual material and the installation context. For an accurate choice, it's always advisable to evaluate physical samples and real references.

Can different materials be combined?

Yes, but with judgment.

IconicWall can combine different finishes on the same wall, as long as the composition remains technically and aesthetically coherent. The point isn't to use many materials, but to choose the right ones.

Accessories and functions

Can IconicWall integrate accessories?

Yes. IconicWall can integrate shelves, storage elements, special panels, supports, light modules and accessories designed for the specific use of the space.

Accessories must be compatible with the chosen technical configuration and the required performance.

Can the accessories be moved?

Yes, when the project includes reconfigurable elements.

Freedom of movement depends on the technical configuration, the weight of the accessories, the type of fastening and the characteristics of the finished wall.

Is it possible to integrate lighting?

Yes, where planned in the project.

Lighting can enhance the composition, create depth, highlight certain modules or improve the perception of the space. Every lighting integration requires a technical and electrical assessment.

Can TVs, outlets or cabling be integrated?

In many cases yes, but these elements must be planned for at the design stage.

TVs, outlets, cabling, light points and other technical elements need to be coordinated with the wall structure, the existing systems and the intended use.

Can IconicWall be used in a kitchen?

Yes, IconicWall can also be designed for kitchens or operational areas, taking into account materials, cleaning, accessories, durability and conditions of use.

In these cases the project must be especially precise, because the wall isn't just decorative: it's used every day.

Costs and project process

How much does IconicWall cost?

The cost depends on dimensions, finishes, complexity of the composition, accessories, special work, lighting and installation method.

This is why there's no single price valid for every project. After an initial assessment, a proposal can be prepared consistent with the space, the goal and the level of customization required.

Can I get an online quote?

You can send an initial request through the site, attaching indicative measurements, photos of the wall, city, type of environment and project goal.

An accurate quote still requires a technical check, because the real wall matters more than any number written in the abstract.

What information is needed to get started?

For an initial assessment, it helps to provide: photos of the wall, indicative dimensions, city or area of installation, type of environment, intended use, any elements to integrate, and visual references.

Can I request just a concept?

Yes. You can start with a consultation, a specific wall or a preliminary concept.

From there, we assess whether to move forward with a technical project, a quote, a prototype or installation.

Does IconicWall make sense for just one wall?

Yes, if that wall has a real role in the space.

IconicWall is at its best when the wall isn't treated as simple filler, but as a design element: a bed headboard, a TV wall, a reception, a kitchen, a display area or a surface that needs to combine function and image.

Contract, hotels and retail

Is IconicWall suitable for hotels?

Yes. IconicWall is especially interesting for hotels, residences and hospitality venues because it allows walls, rooms, corridors, lobbies or common areas to be updated with an orderly, reconfigurable system that's less invasive than many traditional interventions.

It's useful when the image of the space needs to evolve without turning every update into major construction work.

Is it suitable for shops and showrooms?

Yes. In retail and showrooms, IconicWall can become a tool for communication, display and updating the space.

A wall can change function, finish or accessories based on campaigns, products, seasons or new commercial needs.

Can it be used to renovate existing spaces?

Yes. IconicWall is also designed to give new life to existing walls, reducing demolition and invasive work.

It's an interesting solution when you want to improve how a space is perceived without starting from scratch every time.

Can projects be developed for multiple spaces or locations?

Yes. Modular logic makes IconicWall suitable for projects that need to be replicated across multiple environments, rooms, stores or locations.

Each project can maintain overall consistency while still adapting to the measurements and needs of each individual space.
